Clinical Safety and Patient Escalation Protocol (Doc ID: CSP-2025-A)
Medical Director Approval: Dr. Evelyn Vance, MD, Chief Medical Officer

1. Medical Triage Boundaries
Clinical triage assistants and nursing staff are strictly bounded to symptom intake and severity scoring using the Canadian Triage and Acuity Scale (CTAS). Non-physician personnel must never formulate differential diagnoses or suggest prescription pharmaceuticals. Level 1 (Resuscitation) and Level 2 (Emergent) presentations require immediate bedside physician handover within 5 minutes.

2. Zero Unauthorized Dosing Rule
Under no circumstances may any member of clinical nursing or administrative staff alter, adjust, or administer any pharmaceutical dosage without a signed physician prescription or a documented verbal order in an active Code Blue resuscitation. All high-risk medications, including intravenous heparin, insulin infusions, and opioids, require independent double-check verification by two registered nurses before administration.

3. Crisis Escalation Contacts
In the event of an acute behavioral health emergency or active suicide risk:
- Hospital Crisis Response Team: Extension 4499 (Internal Speed Dial 9)
- On-Call Attending Psychiatrist: Direct Beeper 8812
- Regional Psychiatric Assessment Hotline: 1-800-273-8255 (Available 24/7)
For suspected biohazard or hazardous chemical exposure, contact the Hospital Safety Officer immediately via Extension 3311.
