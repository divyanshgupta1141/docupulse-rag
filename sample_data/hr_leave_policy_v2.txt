ACME Corporation Human Resources Leave Policy (Version 2.0)
Effective Date: July 1, 2025

1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees accrue a revised base allowance of 20 annual leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues on the first business day of each month at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Leave requests exceeding three consecutive days must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by the department manager.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick leave days per year for personal medical illness, injury, or to care for an immediate family member. A medical doctor's note is required for any continuous absence exceeding three consecutive business days.

3. Rollover and Forfeiture Rules
Under the revised policy, employees may carry over a maximum of 8 unused annual leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any additional accrued PTO exceeding the 8-day rollover threshold is permanently forfeited on December 31 without cash compensation. Sick leave days do not roll over and reset annually on January 1.

4. Parental and Bereavement Leave
Full-time employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service are eligible for 14 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid days for immediate family members.
